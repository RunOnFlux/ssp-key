# SSP Key v2.1.0

_Release date: 18 August 2026_

Solana mainnet support. Your SSP Key now co-signs Solana transactions —
SOL and SPL tokens (USDC, FLUX) secured by the same 2-of-2 approval flow
as every other chain.

## New

- **Solana mainnet signing** — review and approve SOL and SPL token
  transactions from your vault; nothing moves without your Key's
  signature.
- **Solana chain sync** — activate Solana together with your other chains
  in a single batch-sync approval.

## Changed

- Store listing metadata refreshed.
